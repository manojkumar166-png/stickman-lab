# Free Image Generator — replaced

The standalone image-only workflow has been replaced by the complete **AI Story & Science Video Creator** pipeline. It uses Gemini to write science documentaries or original animated stories, creates scene-specific image prompts, generates and validates every image, adds Edge TTS narration, and renders a final MP4.

Run **Actions → AI Story & Science Video Creator → Run workflow**.

See the [new workflow](../.github/workflows/ai-story-science-video.yml), [pipeline script](../scripts/ai_story_video.py), and [setup guide](../ai-story-science-video/README.md).

Cloudflare remains excluded. OVHcloud and Pollinations are attempted anonymously; no payment credentials, paid fallback, credit purchase, or auto-recharge is configured.
